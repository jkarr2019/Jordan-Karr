Layers of Color

A large abstract painting built up from layer after layer of acrylic, ink, and torn paper, where bold shapes and tiny details compete for your attention.

Wall of Movement

A sweeping mural of interlocking shapes and flowing lines, made with spray paint, house paint, and metallic leaf so the surface shifts as the light changes.

  • Materials: Spray paint, house paint, and metallic leaf
  • Role: Artist
  • Style: Abstract mural

Textured Dreamscape

A dense mixed-media canvas that combines oil pastel, plaster, fabric scraps, and found objects into a surface you want to touch as much as look at.

  • Materials: Oil pastel, plaster, fabric, and found objects
  • Role: Artist
  • Style: Textured abstract
